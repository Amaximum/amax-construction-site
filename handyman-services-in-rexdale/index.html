Handyman Labour Rates

The minimum charge covers the first two hours. Materials and consumable supplies are separate.

General handyman work

$160 minimum for the first two hours, then $60 for each additional hour.

Handyman plumbing and electrical work

$300 minimum for the first two hours, then $99 for each additional hour.

The customer pays for materials and consumable supplies. For larger or combined work, a project price may be discussed after the scope is reviewed and agreed before work begins.

See the hourly-rate guide and project-pricing guide.

What to Include in Your Request

  1. Your full Rexdale civic address, unit where applicable, and who will authorize access.
  2. For door, cabinet, or mounting repairs, identify the affected hardware and show the surrounding surface as well as the damaged part.
  3. Your preferred timing, available replacement parts, and which tasks have priority if the whole list cannot fit one visit.

Confirm the scope, rate, material needs, and availability before booking.

If the request goes beyond hardware or surface repair, check Toronto Building's permit guidance. Changes to openings, structure, or building systems need their own assessment; a short repair list does not establish an exemption.

Frequently Asked Questions

What handyman work can I request in Rexdale?

For a Rexdale request, describe the door, cabinet, mounting, wall, or finish repair and send photos of the affected component. Include the civic address, access arrangements, and any replacement parts. Plumbing needs separate scope review; paid residential electrical work in Ontario requires a Licensed Electrical Contractor, not an ordinary handyman booking.

What are the handyman labour rates?

General handyman work has a $160 minimum for the first two hours, then $60 per additional hour. Plumbing and electrical handyman work has a $300 minimum for the first two hours, then $99 per additional hour. The customer pays for materials and consumable supplies. A negotiated project price may be discussed for larger or combined work after the scope is reviewed and agreed.

Can I ask about a project price?

For larger or combined jobs, a project price may be discussed after the task list and scope are reviewed. Before work begins, confirm the agreed price, included tasks, and any materials charged separately.
