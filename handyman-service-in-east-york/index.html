Reliable Handyman Services in East York

For an East York window or door repair, start with the component that is not working: the opening, frame, latch, hinge, or screen. A public customer review describes window replacement, but that does not mean replacement is the answer to every fault. Send photos and explain how the item operates so adjustment, a replacement part, and a complete replacement can be considered as different requests.

For an East York door or window request, explain whether the problem concerns a hinge, latch, seal, or a proposed replacement. Include a view of the existing opening and identify the building type. A hardware adjustment, a like-for-like replacement, and enlarging an opening are different scopes; list any wall or paint repairs needed around the work.

Toronto's building permit guidance distinguishes structural changes and new openings from certain replacements in existing openings. Its conditions also depend on the building type. Check the actual proposal before booking rather than assuming every door or window task is permit-free. Materials, labour, and timing are confirmed after review.

Can a handyman handle plumbing and electrical work in East York?

Keep fixture plumbing and electrical connections separate from door, wall, and finish repairs. Plumbing needs its own scope review. In Ontario, paid residential electrical work must be contracted through a Licensed Electrical Contractor; verify the business's ECRA/ESA licence and applicable ESA requirements before authorizing it.

Does changing an opening belong in a small repair request?

Describe it separately. Toronto's permit guidance distinguishes certain replacements in existing openings from structural alterations or new or enlarged openings. Include the building type and proposed dimensions; do not assume a replacement and an alteration have the same requirements.
