How to Choose the Best Fence Contractor in Toronto

The difference between a fence that stands straight for 20 years and one that leans within three winters usually comes down to one thing: how the contractor sets the posts. Most homeowners shop on price and a nice-looking portfolio, but the details that actually decide quality — frost-line depth, concrete, permits and warranty — are invisible until something fails. This guide gives you the exact questions to ask before you hire a fence contractor anywhere in Toronto or the GTA.

A fence is one of the few outdoor structures fully exposed to Toronto's freeze-thaw cycles every single winter. The wrong installer can produce a fence that looks perfect on day one and heaves out of the ground by spring. Here is what separates a contractor worth hiring from one to avoid.

Key Takeaways

  • Posts must sit below the 48-inch frost line in concrete or they will heave and lean.
  • Hire a contractor who has installed your specific fence type — wood, vinyl or aluminum.
  • A good contractor knows local height bylaws and pulls permits when required.
  • Get the workmanship warranty in writing before any work starts.
  • Compare line-itemed quotes, not just bottom-line prices.

Post Depth and the Frost Line

This is the single most important factor and the one most cut-rate installers skip. In Toronto, fence posts must be set below the frost line — a minimum of 48 inches deep — and anchored in concrete. Posts set shallower will heave and lean as the ground freezes and thaws. Ask any contractor: "How deep do you set posts, and what concrete do you use?" The right answer is 48 inches or deeper with 60 or 80 lb bags of concrete per post.

Experience With Your Fence Type

Some contractors specialise in wood, others in aluminum or vinyl. A crew that mostly installs chain-link will not produce the cleanest cedar privacy fence. Ask for recent photos and addresses of the specific fence type you want, and confirm they install it regularly. A specialist fence contractor will have no trouble showing you exactly that.

Local Bylaws and Permits

Toronto and GTA municipalities each have fence bylaws — height limits, setback rules and restrictions near corner lots and pools. Side and rear yard fences can usually be up to 2 metres without a permit, while front yard fences are typically capped at 1 metre. A contractor who knows these rules flags issues before they become orders to tear a fence down.

Warranty and Follow-Up

A quality contractor stands behind their work. Ask what workmanship warranty is provided and how they handle a post that shifts or a gate that sags within the first year. Contractors confident in their installation offer warranties without hesitation — vague or verbal-only answers are a warning sign.

Reading the Quote

A trustworthy quote is itemised: materials, post count and depth, concrete, labour, gates and cleanup. Beware quotes that are dramatically lower than the rest — the savings almost always come from shallow posts, fewer posts or cheaper hardware. Compare like for like, not just the bottom line.

Questions to Ask Before You Hire

  • How deep do you set posts, and in concrete?
  • Are you licensed and insured, and can I see proof?
  • Do you handle permits, and do you know my municipality's bylaws?
  • Can I see a fence of this exact type you installed recently?
  • What workmanship warranty do you offer, in writing?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does fence installation cost in Toronto & GTA?

Fence installation in Toronto & GTA ranges from $30 to $100+ per linear foot depending on material and style. Wood (cedar) runs $35–$55/ft, vinyl $45–$75/ft, and aluminum $50–$100/ft. We provide detailed written quotes including materials, labour, and post installation.

Do I need a permit to install a fence in Toronto & GTA?

In Toronto & GTA and most GTA municipalities, fences over 2 metres (6.5 ft) require a building permit. Corner lot fences have additional sightline restrictions. We handle permit applications and ensure your fence meets all local bylaws.

What fence height is allowed in Toronto & GTA?

Standard residential fences in Toronto & GTA can be up to 2 metres (6.5 ft) in side and rear yards without a permit. Front yard fences are typically limited to 1 metre. Corner lots have additional restrictions. We'll advise on the maximum allowable height for your property.

What type of fence gives the most privacy?

Solid board-on-board cedar or vinyl privacy fences with no gaps provide maximum privacy. A standard 6-foot privacy fence is the most common choice in Toronto & GTA. We can add lattice toppers or increase height (with permit) for additional screening.

Wood vs vinyl fence — which is better for Toronto & GTA?

Cedar is the most popular wood choice in Toronto & GTA — naturally rot-resistant and holds stain well. Vinyl (PVC) requires no painting or staining and lasts longer, but costs 30–50% more upfront. We recommend cedar for budget-conscious clients and vinyl for those wanting minimal maintenance.

How long does fence installation take?

A standard residential fence in Toronto & GTA typically takes 1–3 days to install. Longer runs, custom gates, or complex terrain may take 4–5 days. We provide a complete timeline before starting and minimize disruption to your property.

Who owns a boundary fence between neighbours?

In Ontario, boundary fences are generally considered the shared responsibility of both property owners under the Line Fences Act. We can help you document the project properly if cost-sharing with a neighbour is involved.

Can you install gates and custom fence sections?

Yes. We install single and double swing gates, sliding gates, and arched gate tops. Custom fence sections for uneven terrain, slopes, and corners are all part of our standard installation service in Toronto & GTA.

How long will my fence last?

A well-installed cedar fence in Toronto & GTA lasts 15–20 years with regular maintenance (staining every 2–3 years). Vinyl fences last 25–40 years with minimal upkeep. Aluminum fences can last 50+ years. All our fences come with a workmanship warranty.

What is the best fence for a pool enclosure in Toronto & GTA?

Ontario Building Code requires pool enclosures to be at least 1.2 metres (4 feet) high with self-closing, self-latching gates. Aluminum picket fencing is the most common pool fence choice in Toronto & GTA — it's durable, low-maintenance, and meets code requirements.
