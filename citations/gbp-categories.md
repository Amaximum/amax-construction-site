# Google Business Profile — Categories

GBP allows **1 primary** and up to **9 secondary** categories. Choose the primary that matches the
service you want to rank #1 for in the local pack (Maps).

## Recommended primary
- **General Contractor** *(broadest coverage — recommended)*

Alternatives if you want to specialize:
- Construction Company
- Home Builder
- Remodeler

## Recommended secondary (pick up to 9)
1. Home Renovation Contractor *(or "Remodeler" — GBP may show only one)*
2. Bathroom Remodeler
3. Basement Contractor
4. Deck Builder
5. Fence Contractor
6. Landscape Contractor
7. Painter
8. Electrician
9. Plumber

## Categories to add as "Services" instead (not separate categories)
- Carpenter, Demolition Contractor, Excavating Contractor, Christmas Light Installer,
  Canopy Installer, Interlocking Paver Installer — list these in the **Services** tab
  with the descriptions from `services-list.txt`.

## Service area (in GBP setup)
Add cities one-by-one rather than a radius:
Toronto, North York, Etobicoke, Scarborough, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Aurora,
Newmarket, Stouffville, Whitchurch-Stouffville, King City, Mississauga, Brampton,
Oakville, Burlington, Hamilton, Pickering, Ajax, Whitby, Oshawa, Milton, Caledon.

## Attributes to enable
- Identifies as women/family-owned *(if applicable — skip if not)*
- Online estimates: **Yes**
- Onsite services: **Yes**
- Free quote / consultation: **Yes**
- Languages: English *(add others your team speaks)*
- Appointment required: **No** (quote yes, but customers can call without appointment)
