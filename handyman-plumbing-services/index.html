Frequently Asked Questions

How much do handyman plumbing repairs cost in the GTA?

Handyman plumbing work has a $300 minimum for the first two hours, then $99 per additional hour. The customer pays for materials and consumable supplies. Larger or regulated plumbing work is assessed and priced separately.

Are materials included in plumbing handyman pricing?

The customer pays for materials and consumable supplies. If aMaximum purchases them for the job, their cost is charged separately from labour. Confirm material selection and costs before work begins.

Can you respond to urgent plumbing problems?

Call to discuss the issue and current availability. Active flooding or a burst pipe needs an emergency licensed plumber. We can confirm whether our team may assist with a subsequent repair.

When is a licensed plumber involved?

Requests are reviewed individually. Work requiring a licensed plumber must be handled by an appropriately qualified plumbing professional; confirm the responsible provider and applicable requirements before booking.

What are the signs of a hidden water leak?

Watch for unexplained dampness, water stains, or a musty smell. Share what you notice; concealed or slab-leak diagnosis may require a licensed plumbing specialist.

Can you handle plumbing during a home renovation?

We review renovation plumbing requests separately. Work requiring a plumbing trade ticket is scoped and completed by a licensed plumbing professional.
