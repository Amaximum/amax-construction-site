Handyman Hourly Rates in Toronto: Minimums and Extra Hours

For general handyman work, the minimum is $160 for the first two hours, then $60 per additional hour. Plumbing and electrical handyman work has a $300 minimum for the first two hours, then $99 per additional hour. Materials and consumable supplies are paid for by the customer.

What the Two-Hour Minimum Means

The minimum charge covers the first two hours of labour. It applies even if the task itself takes less time. The applicable minimum depends on the type of handyman work; the schedule below distinguishes general work from plumbing and electrical handyman work.

aMaximum Handyman Hourly Rates

Type of workFirst two hoursEach additional hour
General handyman work$160 minimum$60
Plumbing and electrical handyman work$300 minimum$99

The minimum amount covers two hours. Plumbing and electrical work uses the higher schedule; other handyman work uses the general schedule. This pricing applies to handyman work, not automatically to every larger trade or renovation project.

Materials and Consumable Supplies

The hourly amount is for labour. The customer pays for materials and consumable supplies. If we purchase them for the job, their cost is charged separately. For a painting task, for example, the customer pays for the paint and other required supplies; see our painting services.

Examples and Project Pricing

A two-hour electrical handyman visit has a $300 minimum; time after the first two hours is $99 per additional hour. The same schedule applies to plumbing handyman work. Materials remain separate. See our electrical and plumbing service pages for the work details.

For a larger or combined job, we can discuss a negotiated project price after reviewing and agreeing on the scope. This may apply to general handyman, plumbing, or electrical work. It is a price for the agreed work, not a promised discount. Additional work must be priced and approved before it begins. Read more in our guide to handyman project pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the general rate apply to every kind of work?

No. General handyman work and plumbing or electrical handyman work have different minimums and additional-hour rates. A larger trade or renovation project needs its own scope and price; do not assume this schedule covers it.

Where can I see both handyman labour schedules?

General handyman work has a $160 minimum for the first two hours, then $60 per additional hour. Plumbing and electrical handyman work has a $300 minimum for the first two hours, then $99 per additional hour. The customer pays for materials and consumable supplies.

Can I use the minimum appointment for several tasks?

Send all tasks before booking so the scope and applicable rate can be reviewed. Whether several tasks fit the first two hours depends on the work, preparation, and site conditions; the minimum is not a promise to finish a whole list.

How much is three hours of general handyman labour?

For three billable hours at the general schedule, the labour calculation is $160 for the first two hours plus $60 for the third hour, totalling $220. Materials and consumable supplies are separate; confirm the complete quoted terms before booking.

How much is three hours of plumbing or electrical handyman labour?

For three billable hours under that schedule, the calculation is $300 for the first two hours plus $99 for the third hour, totalling $399. Materials are separate. The pricing example does not establish trade eligibility or replace a scope review.

Is a one-hour job half the two-hour minimum?

No. The applicable two-hour minimum remains the minimum charge even if the accepted work takes one hour. Do not divide the minimum to estimate the price of a shorter visit.

How are partial additional hours billed?

Confirm the billing increment in writing before booking. This page states the additional-hour rates, but it does not establish a rounding or partial-hour policy.

Does the hourly rate guarantee appointment availability?

No. A published rate is not a confirmed appointment. Send the address, task list, and preferred timing so scope and current availability can be reviewed separately from the labour schedule.

Are travel, parking, tax, or other charges included?

Ask for the complete written price and any applicable additional charges or taxes. The confirmed information on this page covers labour minimums, additional-hour rates, and customer-paid materials; other terms must not be assumed.

Does agreeing to an hourly rate confirm trade qualifications?

No. Verify the responsible provider, insurance, and applicable qualifications separately. Paid residential electrical work in Ontario must be contracted through a Licensed Electrical Contractor; a price or service label is not evidence of a licence.
