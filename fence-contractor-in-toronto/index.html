Frequently Asked Questions

How much does fence installation cost in Toronto?

A standard 6ft cedar privacy fence costs $40–$65 per linear foot installed. Vinyl is $50–$80/ft. We provide free detailed quotes.

Can you remove and replace my old fence?

Yes — we offer full demolition and disposal of old fencing as part of our installation service.

Do I need a permit for a fence in Toronto?

Fences under 2m (6.5ft) on residential properties generally don't need a permit in Toronto. Taller fences or fences near laneways may require one. We check this for you.

Who is responsible for the fence between neighbours?

In Ontario, the Line Fences Act governs boundary fences. Costs are typically shared. We can advise you on your specific situation.

How long does fence installation take?

Most residential fences are installed in 1–3 days depending on length.
