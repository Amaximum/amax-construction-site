Frequently Asked Questions

How much does electrical handyman work cost in Toronto?

Electrical handyman work has a $300 minimum for the first two hours, then $99 per additional hour. The customer pays for materials and consumable supplies. Larger or regulated electrical work is assessed and priced separately.

Are materials included in electrical handyman pricing?

The customer pays for materials and consumable supplies. If aMaximum purchases them for the job, their cost is charged separately from labour. Confirm material selection and costs before work begins.

Do permit or inspection requirements apply to electrical work?

Confirm notification and inspection requirements with the Electrical Safety Authority and the responsible Licensed Electrical Contractor before work begins. An ESA notification is often called a permit; a handyman service label does not exempt the work from electrical requirements.

Can you install an EV charger in my condo?

A condo EV charger request needs review of the proposed equipment, electrical capacity, parking location, and property-management permissions. Confirm the responsible Licensed Electrical Contractor and required approvals before arranging installation.

What is a panel upgrade and do I need one?

A panel upgrade changes the electrical panel and may involve other service equipment. Whether it is needed depends on the existing equipment, electrical load, and proposed work. Ask a Licensed Electrical Contractor to assess those factors; the age of a home alone does not establish the need.

Is electrical work dangerous to DIY?

Electrical work can present serious safety risks. Do not attempt work outside your qualifications. Check current requirements with the Electrical Safety Authority. For paid residential electrical work in Ontario, hire a Licensed Electrical Contractor, not an individual electrician working independently.
