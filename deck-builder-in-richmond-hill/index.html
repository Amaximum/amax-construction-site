Deck Types We Build in Richmond Hill

We build decks using materials that handle Ontario’s demanding climate while matching your style and budget.

Composite Decking

Composite decking is increasingly favored for its low maintenance requirements and long-lasting durability compared to traditional wood. Made from wood fibers and plastic polymers, composite boards resist rot, fading, and insect damage. Minimal maintenance means no annual staining or sealing.

Best for: Richmond Hill homeowners wanting a durable, low maintenance outdoor space with strong visual appeal.

Pressure Treated Decks

Pressure-treated wood is a popular choice for deck material due to its durability and rot resistance. Chemically treated pine or similar lumber offers affordability while standing up to moisture and insects.

Best for: Budget-conscious homeowners who don’t mind regular maintenance like annual sealing and staining.

Cedar Decks

Cedar is another common material used for decks, known for its natural resistance to decay and insect damage, as well as its attractive appearance. The natural aesthetic of cedar creates a warm, inviting backyard space.

Best for: Homeowners prioritizing natural beauty and authentic wood character with moderate maintenance.

PVC Decking

PVC decking offers maximum weather resistance and longevity. It won’t absorb moisture, making it ideal for areas near pools or high-humidity environments. A good option for homeowners wanting a durable, virtually maintenance-free deck.

Best for: Properties with challenging exposure or homeowners seeking the longest-lasting solution.

Multi-Level Decks

We build decks with pergolas, integrated lighting, privacy screens, and custom railings. Multi-level designs work well for Richmond Hill properties with sloped yards or complex layouts. Our team can create an outdoor living space that includes seating areas, stairs between levels, and features that extend your usable yard.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does deck construction take in Richmond Hill?

A standard permit for deck construction typically takes 4–6 weeks. After permit approval, single-level decks are completed in 3–7 days. Larger multi-level decks with stairs, railings, and pergolas may take 2–3 weeks. Start your permit process at least 3 months before your desired build date.

What permits are required for Richmond Hill deck projects?

Building permits are required if your deck attaches to the house, exceeds 600 mm in height above grade, or covers more than 10 square metres. Even exempt decks must meet zoning setback requirements. Local building codes in Richmond Hill specify regulations regarding height, distance from property lines (typically 1.2 to 2.2 metres), and safety features like railings.

Which deck materials work best for the Ontario climate?

Composite decking and PVC decking handle freeze-thaw cycles best with minimal maintenance. Pressure-treated wood offers durability at a lower cost but requires annual sealing. Cedar provides natural aesthetic appeal with moderate maintenance needs. All materials we use are rated for Ontario’s 100+ annual freeze-thaw cycles.

Do you offer a warranty on deck construction and materials?

Yes. We provide multi-year warranty coverage on workmanship. Material warranties vary by product—many composite brands offer 25–50 year fade and stain warranties. We outline all warranty terms in your contract before work begins.

Can you handle deck repairs and renovations?

Absolutely. We provide deck repair services, including structural repairs, board replacement, refinishing, railing restoration, and safety upgrades. Whether you need minor repairs or a complete deck renovation, our team delivers quality results.
