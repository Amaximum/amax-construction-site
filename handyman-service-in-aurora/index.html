Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you book a handyman in Aurora?

Most Aurora jobs are scheduled within 3–7 business days. Urgent items like a failed door lock, leaking shut-off, or storm damage are slotted in sooner whenever the calendar allows — just flag the urgency when you call.

What handyman services do you offer in Aurora?

We offer a full range of handyman services in Aurora including: drywall repair, painting, furniture assembly, door and window adjustments, caulking, minor plumbing fixes, light fixture installation, shelving, flooring repairs, weatherstripping, and ongoing home maintenance.

How do you price handyman work in Aurora?

For clearly defined tasks (mount a TV, replace a faucet, patch four drywall holes) we quote a flat rate. For open to-do lists we charge hourly with a two-hour minimum. Either way the price is written down and approved before we touch a tool.

Can a handyman handle plumbing and electrical work in Aurora?

We handle minor plumbing — faucet swaps, shut-off valves, toilet parts — and minor electrical such as light fixtures, switches, and outlet replacements that don't require a permit. Anything past that goes to a licensed plumber or electrician on our team.

Do you mount TVs and shelving in Aurora homes?

Yes — flat-screen TVs on drywall, concrete, and brick walls across Aurora. We locate studs or use the correct anchors, level the bracket, and can run cables behind the wall for a clean finish. Floating shelves, picture ledges, and full closet organizers also welcome.

Can you assemble furniture in Aurora?

Yes — IKEA, Wayfair, office furniture, gym equipment, cribs, bunk beds, and complex multi-piece assemblies. We bring the tools, follow the manufacturer steps, and recycle the boxes on the way out.

What about exterior handyman work in Aurora?

Exterior visits in Aurora cover deck-board swaps, fence and gate repair, caulking around windows and doors, gutter clear-outs, weatherstripping, exterior paint touch-ups, and small concrete crack repair to keep water out.

Can you handle multiple small jobs in one visit?

That's our most efficient Aurora booking. A handyman half-day or full-day knocks out an entire to-do list — hang pictures, patch walls, fix squeaky doors, caulk windows, swap hardware, mount the TV — one visit, one invoice.

Do you do drywall repair in Aurora?

Yes — from pinholes to the larger openings a plumber or electrician leaves behind. We patch, tape, mud, sand, and prime so the wall is paint-ready, and match common spray or knockdown textures when needed.
