Frequently Asked Questions

Do you remove the debris and dispose of it?

Yes. Our demolition service in North York includes all debris removal and disposal at approved facilities. We separate materials for recycling where possible (concrete, metal, wood). Disposal fees are included in our written quotes — no surprise charges.

Can you demolish a load-bearing wall?

Yes, but load-bearing wall removal in North York requires a permit and engineering review. We coordinate with structural engineers, obtain the permit, install temporary shoring during the work, and install the required beam to carry the load safely.

How long does demolition take in North York?

Interior room demo in North York: 1–2 days. Full kitchen or bathroom gutting: 2–3 days. Basement demolition: 3–5 days. Full house demolition: 3–7 days. Timeline includes debris removal and site cleanup. We provide a schedule at the start of every project.

Can you remove just one wall or a partial structure?

Yes. Selective demolition is one of our specialties in North York. We can remove a specific wall (load-bearing or non-load-bearing), a section of deck, old bathroom finishes, or any targeted area while protecting the rest of your home.

How much does demolition cost in North York?

Interior room demolition in North York runs $1,500–$5,000 depending on size and disposal requirements. Full kitchen demo: $2,000–$4,000. Deck removal: $1,000–$3,000. Full residential demolition: $15,000–$40,000+. We provide detailed quotes including disposal fees.

Do you offer same-week demolition services in North York?

For smaller jobs in North York (single room demo, deck removal), we can often schedule within 1–2 weeks. For larger projects, scheduling depends on permit timelines and crew availability. Contact us for current availability and fast quotes.

What demolition services do you offer in North York?

We provide interior and exterior demolition services in North York: room gutting, wall removal, bathroom and kitchen demo, basement demolition, deck and fence removal, shed and garage demolition, concrete breaking, and full residential teardowns.

Do I need a permit for demolition in North York?

In North York, permits are required for: structural wall removal, full building demolition, and projects affecting load-bearing elements. Interior soft demolition (gutting finishes, removing non-structural walls) typically doesn't require a permit. We advise on requirements for your project.

How do you handle asbestos and hazardous materials?

For homes built before 1990 in North York, we recommend asbestos testing before demolition begins. If hazardous materials are found, we bring in certified abatement contractors. We do not disturb suspected asbestos materials without proper testing and clearance.

How do you protect the rest of my home during demolition?

We use heavy-duty plastic sheeting, dust barriers, and floor protection in North York homes to contain dust and debris. For exterior demolitions, we protect landscaping and neighbouring properties. Cleanup is part of every job.
