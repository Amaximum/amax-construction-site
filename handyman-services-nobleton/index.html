Handyman Labour Rates

The minimum labour charge covers the first two hours. Materials and consumable supplies are separate.

General handyman work

$160 minimum for the first two hours, then $60 for each additional hour.

Handyman plumbing and electrical work

$300 minimum for the first two hours, then $99 for each additional hour.

The customer pays for materials and consumable supplies. For larger or combined work, a project price may be discussed after scope review and agreed before work begins.

What to Include in Your Request

  1. Your full Nobleton address, postal code, and the specific building or work area involved.
  2. Separate indoor repairs and assembly from outdoor work, plumbing, electrical requests, or changes beyond maintenance.
  3. Identify access arrangements, available materials, and any existing approvals before discussing timing.

Confirm scope, rates, materials, and availability before booking.

For work requiring a King Township permit, use the Township's permit guidance to distinguish submitting an application from receiving an issued approval. Include any relevant permit information when the task list depends on it; an ordinary repair enquiry does not itself establish whether a permit is required.

Frequently Asked Questions

What handyman tasks can I request in Nobleton?

For Nobleton, list indoor repairs and assembly separately from outdoor tasks or proposed alterations. Include the full address, work areas, materials, and any issued approvals so scope and availability can be reviewed. Plumbing requires its own assessment; paid residential electrical work in Ontario must be contracted through a Licensed Electrical Contractor.

What are the handyman labour rates?

General handyman work has a $160 minimum for the first two hours, then $60 per additional hour. Plumbing and electrical handyman work has a $300 minimum for the first two hours, then $99 per additional hour. The customer pays for materials and consumable supplies. A negotiated project price may be discussed for larger or combined work after the scope is reviewed and agreed.

Can I request a project price for several tasks?

For several tasks or a larger job, a project price may be discussed after the requested work is reviewed. Confirm the agreed price, included tasks, and any separately charged materials before work begins.
