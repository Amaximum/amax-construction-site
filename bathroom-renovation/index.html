Planning Your Bathroom Renovation in Toronto

A bathroom renovation is one of the most impactful home improvements you can make — it improves daily life, increases home value, and if done properly, lasts decades. Below is what matters most when planning a bathroom renovation in the GTA — from budget to materials to choosing the right contractor.

Define Your Goals First

Before talking to contractors, clarify what you want to achieve. Is this a cosmetic refresh — new tile, vanity, fixtures? A functional update — adding a shower, enlarging the space? An accessibility renovation — walk-in shower, grab bars? Or a full luxury transformation? Your goal drives the scope, which drives the budget and timeline.

Understanding Bathroom Renovation Costs

In Toronto, bathroom renovation costs range broadly: $8,000–$15,000 for a cosmetic update (new tile, fixtures, vanity, paint); $15,000–$30,000 for a mid-range gut renovation; $30,000–$60,000+ for a full luxury master ensuite. Key cost drivers are tile selection, fixture brands, whether plumbing moves, and whether electrical upgrades are required.

Layout Changes vs. Cosmetic Updates

Moving plumbing dramatically increases renovation cost. If you can achieve your goals by working within the existing plumbing layout — same toilet, sink, and shower/tub locations — you'll save significantly. Moving a toilet even 12 inches requires opening floors, relocating drain and water lines, and potentially permits.

Tile Selection

Tile is the dominant visual element in most bathrooms and the most significant material cost variable. Large-format porcelain (24x24 or larger) looks luxurious and has fewer grout lines to clean, but requires flatter substrate and more skilled installation. Mosaic tiles create texture and interest but are slower to install. Natural stone is beautiful but requires sealing and more maintenance.

The Renovation Process

A full bathroom gut renovation follows this sequence: demo, rough plumbing, rough electrical, inspection, cement board/waterproofing, tile, fixtures, vanity, accessories, final inspection. Our crews complete a standard bathroom in 2–4 weeks, with stages scheduled so trades don't collide and inspections pass the first time.

Choosing a Contractor

Bathroom renovations require coordinated plumbing, electrical, and finishing trades. Hiring a general contractor who manages all trades is simpler than hiring each separately. Verify licensing for plumbers and electricians, ask for bathroom-specific references, and get a detailed written quote before signing.

Permits, Code & Long-Term Care

In Ontario, moving or adding plumbing needs a plumbing permit; structural changes need a building permit. Skipping them can stall a future sale and void insurance after water damage. Proper waterproofing behind tile, GFCI-protected outlets, and code-compliant exhaust ventilation prevent mould. Ask for a written workmanship warranty, then maintain the result: reseal natural-stone surfaces yearly, re-caulk wet joints as they wear, and run the exhaust fan after every shower.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Toronto?

A mid-range bathroom renovation in Toronto typically costs $15,000–$30,000. High-end renovations with custom tile and fixtures range $30,000–$50,000+. We provide free detailed quotes.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

A standard full bathroom renovation takes 2–4 weeks. Timeline depends on scope, permit requirements, and material lead times.

Do I need a permit for a bathroom renovation in Toronto?

Moving or adding plumbing requires a plumbing permit. Structural changes need a building permit. We manage all required permits.

Can you renovate a small bathroom?

Absolutely — we specialize in maximizing small bathroom spaces with smart layout choices and space-saving fixtures.

What is the best tile for a bathroom?

Porcelain is the most durable and water-resistant. Large format tiles (24"x24") make small bathrooms look bigger. We help you choose during the design consultation.
