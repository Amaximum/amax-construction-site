Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer furniture assembly near me?

Assembly requests depend on the item, instructions, condition, and installation location. Share the product details, item count, and photos where useful so we can review the scope and availability.

How much does furniture assembly cost?

Furniture assembly uses the general handyman rate: a $160 minimum for the first two hours, then $60 per additional hour. The customer pays for materials and consumable supplies. A larger or combined scope may be considered for a negotiated project price after review and agreement.

Do you offer professional IKEA furniture assembly?

Share the product name or model, assembly instructions, item count, and photos where useful. We will review whether the assembly request fits our scope and confirm availability before booking.

Can you assemble office furniture?

Office furniture assembly requests can include desks, storage, and workstation pieces. Send the model numbers, quantities, instructions, and any building access restrictions. Wall fixing, electrical connections, and moving existing furniture must be identified separately in the scope.

Can you assemble multiple furniture items in one visit?

Send the complete item list and identify which pieces matter most. We will review assembly complexity, available workspace, missing parts, and any anchoring needs before discussing a visit plan. Several items may require more than one appointment.

Do you assemble Wayfair, Amazon, and Costco furniture?

We review assembly requests for furniture purchased from these retailers. The specific model and manufacturer's instructions determine the work, not the retailer name alone. Send the product link or model, item count, and details of any missing or damaged parts.

How quickly can I book someone to assemble furniture?

Appointment timing depends on the item, location, and current availability. Share the product details, item count, address, and preferred timing so we can confirm available options.

Are your furniture assembly technicians licensed and insured?

Ask for current insurance documentation and identify any building-specific requirements before booking. Assembly, wall anchoring, and regulated trade work are different scopes; confirm the responsible provider and any required qualifications for the actual installation.

How do I request an estimate before booking?

Send the furniture models or product links, quantities, address, and assembly instructions through the booking form. Include existing item condition, access restrictions, and any wall-anchoring request. We will review the details and confirm the proposed scope, pricing, and separately charged materials before booking.

How do you discuss furniture protection during assembly?

Describe existing marks or damage, floor surfaces, and the available assembly area. Agree on handling, floor protection, and packaging removal before work begins. Any wall-anchoring requirements should follow the manufacturer's instructions and be included in the scope.

What if I also need plumbing or electrical handyman service?

Plumbing and electrical requests are reviewed individually. Use the relevant service page and form so the request follows the correct service route. We confirm scope, applicable pricing, and whether qualified trade support or approvals are required before work is booked.

Do you assemble furniture for homes, condos, and offices?

We review requests for all three settings. For a condo or office, include delivery access, elevator arrangements where relevant, permitted work hours, and any property-management rules. For any property, specify the final room and available assembly space.

How do I book furniture assembly?

Use the furniture assembly booking link on this page and send your item list and address. A request is not a confirmed appointment: the scope, applicable price, materials, and available time must first be agreed.
