Selecting Top-Notch Handyman and Contractor Services in Toronto

Hiring the wrong type of pro is one of the most common — and costly — mistakes Toronto homeowners make. Bring in a handyman for a permitted, multi-trade renovation and you'll hit walls fast; hire a full general contractor to mount a TV and you'll overpay. Knowing which professional fits your project keeps it on budget and on schedule. Here's how to choose.

The right choice usually comes down to size, permits and number of trades. A handyman is ideal for quick fixes, while a general contractor manages bigger, permitted builds.

Key Takeaways

  • Handyman: small, single-day, no-permit tasks.
  • General contractor: permitted, multi-trade projects.
  • Roughly $10K+ scope points to a contractor.
  • Structural changes always need a contractor.
  • One company can handle both as your needs change.

When to Hire a Handyman

  • Small repairs — drywall patches, leaky faucets, door adjustments
  • Fixture installation — lights, fans, toilets, hardware
  • Furniture assembly and TV mounting
  • Caulking, painting, and touch-up work
  • Tasks that take less than a day and don't require permits

When to Hire a General Contractor

  • Renovations requiring permits — basement, bathroom, kitchen, deck
  • Projects involving multiple trades — framing, electrical, plumbing, tile
  • Projects over $10,000 in scope
  • Structural changes — wall removal, additions, load-bearing modifications
  • Projects with defined scope, timeline, and budget accountability requirements

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a handyman and a general contractor?

A handyman handles small repairs and installations that take less than a day and don't require permits. A general contractor manages larger, multi-trade projects that require permits, coordination and budget accountability.

Do handyman jobs need permits?

Typically no. Handyman tasks like drywall patches, fixture installs and touch-up work don't require permits. Permitted work — basements, bathrooms, kitchens, decks — calls for a general contractor.

When should I hire a general contractor?

Hire a general contractor for renovations that need permits, involve multiple trades, exceed roughly $10,000 in scope, or include structural changes like wall removal or additions.

Can one company do both?

Yes. aMaximum Construction provides both handyman services for smaller tasks and full general contracting for larger renovations — same team, same standards.

Is a handyman cheaper than a contractor?

For small, single-trade tasks a handyman is usually more cost-effective. For larger, multi-trade or permitted projects, a general contractor delivers better value through coordination and accountability.

Who do I call for a multi-trade renovation?

Call a general contractor. They coordinate framing, electrical, plumbing and tile under one project manager, handle permits and stand behind the finished result.
