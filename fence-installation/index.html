Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes. We provide free measurement and written quotes for Fence Installation projects for homeowners planning a new fence around the yard or wider outdoor space.

Do you handle permits and code requirements?

We build and install to Ontario Building Code requirements and can advise on permits and inspection requirements based on your municipality. Many homeowners should also confirm who is responsible for obtaining local building permits before work starts.

How long does a typical project take?

Fence installation typically takes a few days to a few weeks. Timelines vary by scope and materials, including the fencing option selected, whether it is a wood fence, vinyl fencing, chain link fence, aluminum fencing, cedar fence, or wrought iron. After a site visit, we provide a clear schedule before work begins, giving you added peace throughout the project.

Do you offer warranties or other outdoor projects?

Yes. We provide workmanship warranties on our fence installation projects and can discuss warranty coverage during your consultation. We also build decks and other outdoor structures as part of our residential and commercial construction services.

How do I choose the right fence for my property?

The right fence depends on your layout, privacy goals, budget, and overall fencing needs, and we help you compare fence materials for the best fit. Ask for at least three client references when comparing contractors. A chain link fence is often the lowest priced option for practical boundaries. Vinyl fencing is low maintenance, nearly maintenance free, and stands up well to tough weather. Aluminum fencing offers strength, rust resistance, and durability with very little upkeep. A wood fence can be pressure treated or a cedar fence, and wood fences are commonly graded as 1st grade and 2nd grade; cedar is valued for its natural beauty plus resistance to decay and insects. Wood pricing has risen in recent years, so vinyl can sometimes cost less on certain projects. Basic PVC products are usually thinner and less expensive, while reinforced or higher-end PVC can offer better warranties and added performance; warranty coverage can differ by product and installation, including manufacturer warranties on materials and workmanship warranties on installations. Wrought iron and ornamental steel are more premium choices and usually need more maintenance than vinyl or aluminum. We also recommend annual inspections, cleaning every few years, resealing or painting wood when needed, checking hardware for quality to help prevent fence repair or larger repair issues, and making sure post depth is typically about half the height of the fence so the finished installation can stand up over time. If you have an existing fence, we can assess its condition, recommend gate installation where needed, and advise whether to replace it or repair it.
