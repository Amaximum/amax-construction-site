Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Handyman services in Markham.

What details should I include with a Markham handyman request?

Send the full Markham address, task list, photos where useful, and any access details. This helps us review whether the work fits our handyman scope.

What should be confirmed before a handyman job starts?

Confirm the included tasks, exclusions, applicable labour charges, material costs, and how added work will be handled. Agree on the complete scope and price before work begins.

Can several small jobs be reviewed for one visit?

Share the full list and address. We will review whether the tasks can be included in one visit and confirm the scope, applicable price, and schedule before booking.

What information helps assess drywall repair?

Send photos, approximate dimensions, and the known cause. If moisture is present, resolve the source before cosmetic patching; a photo alone cannot confirm concealed damage or an exact finish match.

How are plumbing and electrical requests reviewed?

Describe the plumbing and electrical tasks separately. Qualifications and approvals depend on the work. Paid residential electrical work in Ontario must be contracted through a Licensed Electrical Contractor; confirm applicable ESA requirements.

What should I share for furniture assembly?

Share the furniture models, item count, assembly instructions if available, and where each piece will go. Note any wall-anchoring request so it can be reviewed separately.

What details help assess a TV mounting request?

Send the TV and mount model, wall type, intended location, dimensions, and any cable-management request. Concealed electrical work has separate qualification and approval requirements.

Can you respond to urgent repair requests?

Urgent requests still require a scope and availability review. For an active leak or immediate safety risk, contact an appropriate emergency or qualified trade provider; a booking request does not confirm urgent response.

Can I request exterior repairs in Markham?

Share the address and photos of the existing feature and describe the requested repair. Exterior changes or work on heritage properties may need separate municipal review; check applicable Markham requirements before proceeding.

How can I verify licences, insurance, or warranty terms?

Ask for the documents and terms relevant to the specific provider and task. Do not assume licensing, insurance, permits, or warranty coverage from a general handyman booking; confirm who will perform regulated work and any project-specific warranty in writing.
