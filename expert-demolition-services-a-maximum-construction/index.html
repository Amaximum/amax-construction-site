Our Approach to Demolition

Every demolition project begins with a site assessment: identifying structural elements to preserve, locating utilities to disconnect or protect, and testing for hazardous materials (asbestos, lead paint) in older structures. Only after this assessment does demolition begin. This order matters — cutting into an unknown wall is how people hit live wiring, water lines or asbestos-wrapped pipe.

Containment and Dust Control

Interior demolition in occupied homes requires careful dust and debris containment. We use plastic sheeting barriers, negative air pressure when needed, and daily cleanup to minimize disruption to the rest of your home during renovation projects. Containment is the difference between demolition you barely notice and a fine layer of grit on every surface in the house.

Responsible Disposal

Demolition generates significant waste. aMaximum Construction arranges proper disposal — separating materials for recycling where possible (concrete, metal, wood) and ensuring hazardous materials are disposed of through certified channels. Diverting clean material from landfill is both better for the environment and often cheaper than mixed-waste tipping fees.

Integrated With Renovation Projects

When demolition is the first phase of a larger demolition and renovation project, proper execution here saves time and money throughout. We demolish exactly what needs to come out, protect what stays, and leave a clean site ready for the next phase of work — no surprises for the trades that follow.

What Demolition Costs

Pricing depends on scope and disposal. Interior strip-outs of a single room or basement typically run $3,000–$10,000. Structural or whole-house demolition can reach $15,000–$40,000+, and asbestos or lead abatement adds to the total when older materials are present. A site assessment gives you a firm, itemized number before any work starts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does demolition cost in Toronto?

Interior strip-outs typically run $3,000–$10,000 depending on size and disposal, while structural or whole-house demolition can reach $15,000–$40,000+. Asbestos abatement adds to the total.

Do I need a permit for demolition?

Interior non-structural demolition often does not need a standalone permit, but structural removal, full-building demolition and anything affecting load-bearing elements do. We confirm permit needs before starting.

How do you handle asbestos during demolition?

Older structures are tested first. If asbestos or lead is found, it is removed by certified abatement crews through legal disposal channels before general demolition begins.

How long does interior demolition take?

Most interior demolition for a single room or basement takes one to three days, plus time for hazardous-material testing or abatement if required.

Do you clean up and dispose of debris?

Yes. We arrange bins, separate recyclable materials, and leave a clean, swept site ready for the next phase of work.

Can demolition be done while I live in the home?

Yes. We use plastic barriers, negative air pressure and daily cleanup to contain dust so the rest of your home stays livable during the work.

What demolition services do you offer in Toronto & GTA?

We provide interior and exterior demolition services in Toronto & GTA: room gutting, wall removal, bathroom and kitchen demo, basement demolition, deck and fence removal, shed and garage demolition, concrete breaking, and full residential teardowns.

Do I need a permit for demolition in Toronto & GTA?

In Toronto & GTA, permits are required for: structural wall removal, full building demolition, and projects affecting load-bearing elements. Interior soft demolition (gutting finishes, removing non-structural walls) typically doesn't require a permit. We advise on requirements for your project.

How much does demolition cost in Toronto & GTA?

Interior room demolition in Toronto & GTA runs $1,500–$5,000 depending on size and disposal requirements. Full kitchen demo: $2,000–$4,000. Deck removal: $1,000–$3,000. Full residential demolition: $15,000–$40,000+. We provide detailed quotes including disposal fees.

How do you handle asbestos and hazardous materials?

For homes built before 1990 in Toronto & GTA, we recommend asbestos testing before demolition begins. If hazardous materials are found, we bring in certified abatement contractors. We do not disturb suspected asbestos materials without proper testing and clearance.

How long does demolition take in Toronto & GTA?

Interior room demo in Toronto & GTA: 1–2 days. Full kitchen or bathroom gutting: 2–3 days. Basement demolition: 3–5 days. Full house demolition: 3–7 days. Timeline includes debris removal and site cleanup. We provide a schedule at the start of every project.

Do you remove the debris and dispose of it?

Yes. Our demolition service in Toronto & GTA includes all debris removal and disposal at approved facilities. We separate materials for recycling where possible (concrete, metal, wood). Disposal fees are included in our written quotes — no surprise charges.

Can you remove just one wall or a partial structure?

Yes. Selective demolition is one of our specialties in Toronto & GTA. We can remove a specific wall (load-bearing or non-load-bearing), a section of deck, old bathroom finishes, or any targeted area while protecting the rest of your home.

How do you protect the rest of my home during demolition?

We use heavy-duty plastic sheeting, dust barriers, and floor protection in Toronto & GTA homes to contain dust and debris. For exterior demolitions, we protect landscaping and neighbouring properties. Cleanup is part of every job.

Yes, but load-bearing wall removal in Toronto & GTA requires a permit and engineering review. We coordinate with structural engineers, obtain the permit, install temporary shoring during the work, and install the required beam to carry the load safely.
