Our Demolition Process

Step 1: Free Site Assessment

Every project starts with a free consultation and site assessment. We review the project details, inspect the building, discuss your demolition needs, and explain what must happen before work begins.

Our team checks for unstable structures, adjacent structures, utility lines, access issues, load-bearing walls, buried services, asbestos risk, lead risk, and contamination concerns. Asbestos testing is strongly recommended before any demolition, especially in buildings constructed before the 1990s, to identify hazardous materials that may be present.

Step 2: Detailed Quote & Planning

You receive a written estimate with transparent pricing, schedule details, disposal expectations, and the specific requirements for the job. We explain permit needs, inspections, utility disconnection, dumpster placement, debris handling, and any hazardous materials concerns.

An Environmental Building Audit is required if an assessment indicates the potential presence of asbestos, lead, or other toxic substances before a permit can be approved. If hazardous materials like asbestos are found during testing, certified abatement must be completed prior to demolition to protect workers and the public. Professional asbestos removal services are essential for ensuring compliance with safety regulations and minimizing health risks during demolition projects.

Step 3: Safe Demolition Execution

Our experienced crew arrives with proper safety equipment, safety barriers, dust protection, tools, and advanced demolition equipment suited to the job. We follow strict adherence to safety protocols to protect workers, customers, neighbours, and surrounding properties. Selective demolition is used when parts of the building must be preserved, valuable materials can be recovered, or the next project requires careful prep.

Step 4: Complete Cleanup & Disposal

After demolition, we remove debris, sweep the job site, coordinate bins, sort recyclable materials, and send waste to proper disposal facilities. Responsible debris removal keeps the site safe and ready for construction, renovation, excavation, or inspection.
