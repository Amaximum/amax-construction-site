Fence Types We Install in Aurora

Wood Fences

Wood remains the most popular choice for residential fencing due to its timeless, natural look that blends seamlessly with various landscape designs. We install:

Cedar Fencing – Naturally resistant to decay and insects, cedar lasts 20-30 years with proper care.

Pressure Treated Wood – Cost-effective option with a 15-20 year lifespan when maintained.

Custom Wood Designs – Picket fence styles, horizontal slats, board-on-board patterns

Wood fences require specific maintenance, including staining or sealing every 2-3 years to protect against moisture and UV damage. We advise every client on proper care.

Privacy Fences

Privacy fences are typically tall and solid, designed to block prying eyes and create a secluded outdoor space. Aurora allows rear and side yard fences up to 2.0 metres—perfect for keeping pets safe and deterring potential intruders.

Options include:

Solid panel privacy screens

Lattice top designs for decorative appeal

Composite materials combining wood aesthetics with plastic durability

Vinyl Fencing

Vinyl fencing is known for its durability and low maintenance, making it an excellent choice for homeowners who want a long-lasting solution without the upkeep associated with wood. Vinyl resists rot, rust, and pests while lasting 25-30+ years.

Ornamental Iron and Aluminum

Ornamental iron and aluminum fences provide elegance and security, offering a sophisticated look while allowing for open views of gardens and landscapes. Wrought iron adds classic curb appeal to any home's exterior.

Chain Link Fencing

Chain-link fencing is a cost-effective and durable option for securing properties, often used in both residential and commercial settings. Properly tensioned chain link with concreted posts lasts 20-30 years.

Commercial Fencing

We serve commercial property owners needing security fences, decorative boundaries, and professional installations that meet business requirements. Our residential and commercial needs coverage makes us the best bet for property managers.

Factors Affecting Cost

The cost of installing a fence can vary significantly based on the type of materials used, with options ranging from wood and vinyl to metal and composite materials. Additional factors influencing the cost of a new fence include:

Fence Height and Length – More linear feet means higher material and labor costs.

Property Terrain – Rocky or sloped yards add 15-50% to excavation costs.

Gate Requirements – Each walk-gate or drive-gate adds $300-$800, depending on size.

Old Fence Removal – Demolition and disposal typically runs $3-$5 per linear foot

Design Complexity – Custom patterns, mixed materials, or additional features increase pricing.

Seasonal Considerations

Peak demand runs from April through June when Aurora homeowners want installations completed for summer. Scheduling in the fall (October-November) often means faster project starts. Ground freezes in winter make installation difficult.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does fence installation cost in Aurora?

Expect CAD $40-$95 per linear foot for typical residential fence installation in Aurora. A 100-foot privacy fence generally costs $4,000-$9,500 installed, depending on materials. Factors influencing the cost of a new fence include the height, length, and complexity of the design, as well as local labor rates and any necessary permits. We provide free quotes with detailed breakdowns.

Do I need a permit for fence installation in Aurora?

Fences in Aurora generally do not require a permit unless they serve as a swimming pool enclosure. However, all fences must comply with Aurora’s Fence By-law: front yard fences limited to 1.2 metres, side and rear yard fences up to 2.0 metres. Homeowners should consider local regulations and zoning laws before installing a fence, as these can dictate height restrictions and material guidelines.

How long does a fence installation project take?

Most residential fence installation projects complete within 1-2 weeks from initial consultation to finished fence. Projects requiring permits or involving custom designs may take 3-4 weeks. We communicate timeline updates throughout your project.

What fence materials work best for Ontario weather?

Fencing materials must withstand high winds and freeze-thaw cycles typical in the Ontario climate, making materials like pressure-treated wood, vinyl, or chain link suitable choices. Cedar offers natural rot resistance. Regular maintenance, such as inspecting for damage and applying protective coatings, can significantly extend the lifespan of a fence, especially in harsh weather conditions.

Do you offer a warranty on fence installation and materials?

Contractors should provide a warranty on labor and materials, typically lasting from 1 to 5 years, to protect the homeowner’s investment. We stand behind our work with craftsmanship warranties and can explain manufacturer warranties on specific materials. References from past clients and examples of previous work are available upon request.
