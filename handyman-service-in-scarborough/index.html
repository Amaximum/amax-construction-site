How quickly can you book a handyman in Scarborough?

What should a Scarborough wall-repair request show?

Include a close-up, a wider view, approximate dimensions, and the known cause of the damage. Tell us whether moisture is active and whether you want patching alone or painting as well. Identify the rooms involved so preparation and any return visit can be assessed.

How do you price handyman work in Scarborough?

General handyman work has a $160 minimum for the first two hours, then $60 per additional hour. Plumbing and electrical handyman work has a $300 minimum for the first two hours, then $99 per additional hour. The customer pays for materials and consumable supplies. A negotiated project price may be discussed for larger or combined work after the scope is reviewed and agreed.

Can a handyman handle plumbing and electrical work in Scarborough?

Identify plumbing repairs and electrical tasks separately from drywall finishing, especially where another repair must be completed first. Plumbing needs individual review. Paid residential electrical work in Ontario requires a Licensed Electrical Contractor; confirm the business's ECRA/ESA licence and relevant ESA requirements.

Do you mount TVs and shelving in Scarborough homes?

Mounting depends on the item, wall construction, mounting hardware, access, and any requested cable work. Share photos and details so we can review the scope and applicable requirements before booking.

Can you assemble furniture in Scarborough?

Assembly requests depend on the item, instructions, condition, and installation location. Share the product details, number of items, and photos where useful so we can review scope and availability.

What about exterior handyman work in Scarborough?

Some exterior requests may fit the handyman scope, subject to access, safety, weather, materials, and applicable requirements. Share the location and photos so we can review the request before booking.

Can you handle multiple small jobs in one visit?

You may share several tasks in one request. We will review which items fit the handyman scope, confirm the applicable rate and expected time, and check appointment availability. Whether all requested work fits one visit depends on the tasks and site conditions.

Can a stained wall be patched before the leak is resolved?

A cosmetic patch does not solve the source of moisture. Explain whether the leak has been diagnosed and stopped before requesting finishing work. Drying, substrate condition, damage extent, and the desired paint finish determine whether the wall is ready for a repair assessment.
