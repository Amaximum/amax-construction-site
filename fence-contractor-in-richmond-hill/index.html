Top 8 Fence Types We Install in Richmond Hill

1. Wood Privacy Fences

Wood fences remain the most popular choice for residential fencing due to their timeless, natural look that blends well with various landscape designs. 2. Vinyl Fences

Vinyl fencing is known for its durability and low maintenance, as it does not rot, warp, or require painting, making it a popular choice for homeowners. Chain link fencing is often considered a practical and cost-effective solution for securing residential properties, especially for large backyards or for keeping pets contained. 5. Composite Fencing

6. Aluminum Fencing

Aluminum fencing is a durable option that does not rust, making it suitable for various climates, and it typically requires minimal maintenance due to its powder-coated finish. Safety-compliant pool fencing protects your family while meeting Richmond Hill’s strict requirements. Common specialties for fence contractors include pool safety fencing and privacy fences.

Timeline and Pricing Factors for Richmond Hill Fence Installation

Installation Timeline

Most residential fence installation projects in Richmond Hill take 1-3 days once materials arrive and permits are secured. Complex custom designs, large commercial properties, or challenging terrain may extend this timeline.

Factors affecting installation speed include:

  • Fence length and complexity
  • Gate quantity and style
  • Terrain conditions (slopes, obstacles)
  • Weather during the installation period

Pricing Factors

Prices for fence installation in Ontario typically range from $40 to $95 per linear foot, fully installed. Detailed estimates from contractors should break down costs for materials, labor, and timelines.

Key factors influencing your fence installation cost:

  • Fence type and material: Wood, vinyl, aluminum, and composite materials carry different price points
  • Height and linear footage: Taller and longer fences require more materials and labour
  • Terrain challenges: Slopes, rocks, or roots increase installation complexity
  • Gate requirements: Style, size, and hardware affect total cost
  • Permit and survey costs: Richmond Hill permits and property line verification
  • Existing fence removal: Removal of an old fence typically adds $6 to $15 per linear foot to the total installation cost

Seasonal Considerations

Late spring through early summer offers ideal installation conditions once the ground has thawed. We maintain flexible scheduling to accommodate Richmond Hill homeowners throughout the building season.

Collect at least three written quotes that detail costs for materials, labor, permits, and old fence removal. Request proof of Workplace Safety and Insurance Board (WSIB) coverage and general liability insurance, with a minimum coverage of $2M-$5M recommended.

What fence materials work best in Ontario weather?
