Questions to Ask Before Choosing a General Contractor

What does a general contractor do?

A general contractor manages the connected parts of a residential construction project, including scope, scheduling, permits, trade coordination, site decisions, quality checks, and final completion.

How are general contractor fees structured?

Fees depend on project scope, coordination responsibility, materials, permits, and the agreed contract structure. A written estimate should explain what is included and how approved changes affect the budget.

When should a nearby contractor discuss permits?

Permit requirements should be discussed during project assessment whenever the scope may involve structural changes, additions, new systems, or other regulated work. The contractor should make the application and inspection responsibilities clear.

How do I compare general contractors near me?

Compare the written scope, project experience, insurance, permit responsibility, communication process, schedule, payment milestones, change-order terms, and warranty documentation rather than comparing price alone.

How is a complex project schedule developed?

The schedule depends on scope, permits, materials, access, trade sequence, inspections, and approved changes. A responsible contractor explains the milestones and dependencies before construction begins.

What should be included in the written scope?

The written scope should describe the work, materials, responsibilities, assumptions, schedule, payment milestones, change-order process, warranty terms, and items that still require confirmation.

Can one contractor coordinate multiple trades?

Yes. A general contractor can coordinate the sequence and handoffs between the trades involved, keeping responsibilities, access, materials, inspections, and decisions connected.

How are project changes documented?

Changes should be described in writing with their cost, schedule impact, and approval status before the related work proceeds.

Who coordinates permits and inspections?

The project plan should identify who prepares documents, submits applications, books inspections, and follows up on outstanding requirements.

What should I ask about warranty terms?

Ask for written workmanship terms, the reporting process for outstanding items, response expectations, and the way manufacturer warranties are handled.

What project scope can a general contractor coordinate?

A general contractor can coordinate a complex residential scope that involves multiple trades, several phases, permits, inspections, and one accountable project structure.

How do I request a project assessment?

Share the property, project goals, known constraints, and the type of coordination you need. The next step is a scope review before a written estimate is prepared.

How does permit coordination work?

Permit coordination starts by identifying the work that may require approval, the documents needed, the responsible parties, and the inspection milestones connected to the construction schedule.

How long can a complex project take?

Timing depends on scope, permits, materials, access, trade sequence, inspections, and approved changes. A reliable contractor provides a project schedule after the scope is understood.
