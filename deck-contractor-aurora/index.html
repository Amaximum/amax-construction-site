Composite Decking

Composite decking is a popular choice for homeowners who want a composite deck with low maintenance and durability, as it does not warp, rot, or fade like traditional wood decking. We install Trex, TimberTech, and other premium brands.

Made from wood fibers and recycled plastic, composite resists fading, staining, and scratching, and lasts 25–30 years. A composite or PVC surface is popular in Aurora for resisting moisture without annual staining.

Natural Wood Decks

Natural wood and wooden decks are a classic choice, often built with pressure-treated lumber, cedar wood, or cedar decking for homeowners who prefer traditional wooden decking.

Western Red Cedar is naturally resistant to rot and insects, but requires regular sealing. The natural beauty of cedar makes it a favorite for homeowners who appreciate traditional aesthetics.

Pressure-treated lumber is cost-effective but requires high maintenance to prevent warping in the local climate. Pressure-treated wood remains incredibly durable when properly maintained.

PVC Decking

Vinyl decks are made from synthetic plastic and are known for being low-maintenance, although they may not be as durable as wood or composite options. PVC decking works well for homeowners who prioritize easy care and save time on maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does deck construction take in Aurora?

Most standard deck projects take 1-2 weeks for planning through installation. Multi-level or composite decks may take 2-4 weeks. Weather, permit approval times, and material availability affect your timeline. We provide realistic schedules during your free estimate.

What permits are required for deck building in Aurora?

You must obtain a building permit if your deck is attached to the house or more than 2 feet (0.6 metres) above the finished grade. There are two mandatory inspections in Aurora: one before backfilling foundations (piers) and one upon completion. We handle all permit applications and inspection scheduling.

Which deck materials work best for Ontario weather?

Composite decking is a popular low-maintenance option that combines wood fibers and plastic, offering durability and resistance to fading, staining, and scratching. Composite and PVC decking handle freeze-thaw cycles without warping. Cedar performs well with regular sealing. Pressure treated lumber works at a lower budget but needs more maintenance.

How much does a custom deck cost in Aurora?

Deck construction costs in Aurora range from $40-$55 per square foot for pressure-treated wood, $55-$70 for cedar, $75-$90 for composite, and $80-$100 for PVC decking. Final costs depend on size, height, railings, and custom features. A typical Aurora deck project runs $8,850 on average, with projects ranging from $4,200 to $18,600 based on scope.

Do you offer warranty on deck construction work?

Yes. We provide workmanship warranties covering installation quality, plus manufacturer warranties on decking boards. Premium composite decking often carries 25-30 year material warranties. Interview at least three contractors to compare pricing and expertise. A trustworthy builder will offer references to check.
