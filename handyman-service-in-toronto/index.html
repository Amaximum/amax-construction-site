Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a handyman cost in Toronto?

Handyman services typically start at $150–$250 for a first hour. Most small jobs are completed in 2–4 hours. We provide fixed quotes before starting any work.

Do you have a minimum charge?

We have a minimum service call of $150. Most jobs are quoted at a fixed price.

Are your handymen licensed and insured?

Yes — all our handymen are insured and our company carries $2M liability insurance and WSIB coverage.

Can you do multiple small jobs in one visit?

Absolutely — our handymen can tackle a list of small tasks in a single visit, making it efficient and cost-effective.

How quickly can you come?

We typically schedule within 2–5 business days. For urgent requests, call us directly at (647) 967-8555.
